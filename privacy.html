Privacy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

The game runs in your browser and has no accounts.

Visitor analytics. We count visits to this website with Umami, running on our own server, so nothing goes to an analytics company. It sets no cookies and does not follow you to other sites. For each visit it records the page, the referring site, your browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language and an approximate location (country, region and city, worked out from your IP address; the address itself is not stored). Visit records are deleted after 24 months.
